# Credit cards in LabBank

Two optional retail products share `CreditCardsGen`, a subclass of `LoansFloatGen`:

* 2200 / `credit_card_revolver`: interest-bearing revolving drawings.
* 2210 / `credit_card_transactor`: statement balances paid in full, interest-free.

The account is revolving; its valuation run-off is not an amortising term-loan
contract. `balance_amt` is drawn principal. `credit_limit = drawn / utilization`
and `undrawn_amt = credit_limit - drawn`. Undrawn amounts do not increase total
assets. Constant utilization links the facility size to the product weight in
the optimizer. Account-level limits and undrawn amounts are stored in
`schemat.loans` and summed into `sched.loans`.

## Business and risk treatment

Monthly principal repayment is a fraction of opening drawn balance, with
interest paid separately. This is an assumed behavioural principal repayment,
NOT a minimum total payment including interest. There is no capitalized
unpaid interest, delinquency migration or negative amortisation. Transactors
repay 100% in one month. Revolvers use geometric run-off with any residual paid
at the explicit horizon (`maturity`, e.g. 10Y). That horizon is a modelling
assumption, not a contractual expiry. At 5% monthly repayment, the 10Y terminal
residual is approximately 0.22% of opening principal.

The existing rate model handles indexed or administered pricing through beta:
`client_rate = clip(beta * index + margin, client_floor, client_cap)`.
Both card rows use rate_type V for integration; beta may be zero. Transactors
must set beta=0 and client_cap=0. Revolvers require explicit tariff inputs.
Example rates are illustrative and caps must reflect the relevant country's
contract and applicable law. Payment and resetting are monthly in the example.

EVE uses existing-drawing run-off, without new spending. NII uses the existing
constant-balance-sheet replacement mechanism to renew principal at the same
product tariff. This approximates new card spending; there is no transaction
ledger or gross spend/redraw simulation. Existing and renewed transactor
balances remain interest-free even under shocks. Fees belong to economic
profit (`fee_unit_rate`), not interest cash flows. This is a net annual fee
yield per drawn unit; turnover, interchange, loyalty and fraud costs are not
individually simulated. Defaults/losses use the existing annual PD/LGD expected
loss model, not IFRS 9 staging or default cash-flow recovery paths.

Undrawn facilities enter contingent liquidity and credit risk:

* LCR additional outflow = undrawn * `card_undrawn_lcr`.
* NSFR additional RSF = undrawn * `card_undrawn_rsf`.
* EAD = drawn + undrawn * `card_undrawn_ccf`.
* RWA = EAD * `rwa_weight`; annual EL = EAD * PD * LGD.

Fast parameters fold these into factors per drawn unit, preserving the
optimizer's linear scaling and leaving the original PD unchanged. All card
run-off inflows are excluded from LCR as a conservative modelling policy
because repayments may be offset by continuing spending. This is not a claim
that regulation mandates excluding every card inflow. No undrawn contractual
option valuation or shock-dependent future utilization is implemented; the
contingent factors are the lab's approximation, not a complete production
IRRBB optionality model. Utilization is held constant in optimization.

## Regulatory sources

* [EBA Q&A 2020_5159](https://www.eba.europa.eu/single-rule-book-qa/qna/view/publicId/2020_5159):
  undrawn credit-card/overdraft limits are loan commitments (off balance sheet).
* [LCR Delegated Regulation 2015/61, Article 31](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32015R0061):
  5% drawdown for qualifying retail committed facilities. Cancellability and
  legal conditions affect classification; the example conservatively uses 5%.
* [EU 2024/856, Articles 3–4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2024/856/oj/eng):
  EVE run-off and NII constant balance sheet, commercial margins and optionality.
* [EBA IRRBB/CSRBB guidelines](https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/single-rulebook/regulatory-activities/supervisory-review-and-evaluation-process-srep-0?version=2022):
  governance and behavioural assumptions for interest-sensitive exposures.

PD, LGD, CCF, RSF and pricing examples are editable laboratory assumptions,
not regulatory classifications or an institution-specific calibration.

## Input examples and activation

`docs/credit_card_input_examples.json` contains full `bs_structure` and
`interest_rt` rows for EUR and PLN, generated by
`python -m labbank_common.card_examples`. Copy the relevant rows into the two
workbooks. Add the five `card_*` columns to `bs_structure`; ordinary products
may leave them blank. Existing portfolios and output NPZ files are unchanged.

The example allocates 0.5 and 0.25 **percentage points of the full balance**
(the existing project convention: 2 * total_assets), hence 1% and 0.5% of
assets. Reduce another asset allocation by 0.75 full-balance points so the
asset total remains 50. Do not add an asset row for the undrawn commitment.

| New bank_data column | Meaning | Revolver | Transactor |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| card_utilization | Drawn / limit | 0.40 | 0.25 |
| card_monthly_principal_rate | Principal repayment / opening drawn | 0.05 | 1.00 |
| card_undrawn_lcr | Contingent 30-day drawdown factor | 0.05 | 0.05 |
| card_undrawn_rsf | Undrawn required funding factor | 0.05 | 0.05 |
| card_undrawn_ccf | Undrawn credit conversion factor | 0.40 | 0.40 |

All these values are fractions. `margin_pct` in interest_rt is in percentage
points (12 means 12%); client floors/caps are decimals (0.20 means 20%).
amort_type=3 selects card run-off. Do not add a second loan prepayment model
for cards: monthly repayment already models their run-off.

Run the usual balance-generation stage (mode 0 rebuilds loan schema), followed
by add_data, cash flows, IRRBB, liquidity and optimize_prep. Existing databases
need schema regeneration before add_data because loans gain three columns.
Historical market data and the existing curves are reused. The sandbox
discovers the new codes from rebuilt NPZ files and the product-label map.

The test suite exercises generation, balance/limit reconciliation, complete
run-off, interest-free transactors, behavioural stream retention, baseline and
up-shocked EVE/NII, and contingent liquidity/credit exposure for EUR and PLN.
The SQL Server pipeline must be run separately to regenerate production data.
